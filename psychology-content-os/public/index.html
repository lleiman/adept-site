Architecture

How the machine works

01
English Internet

Reddit, X, YouTube and public Telegram are scanned for psychology topics with real velocity.

02
Trend Radar

Signals are reduced to 5–7 themes with evidence, mechanics, and replication notes.

03
Core thesis

The strongest themes are translated into your own point of view: not summary, but interpretation.

04
Content pack

One thesis becomes Telegram, Reel/Short, X, YouTube long-form outline and a Reddit discussion angle.

05
Human approval

You edit the thesis, record a voice/video take, and approve. Full autopublishing comes only after the voice is stable.

06
Feedback

Performance data is used to learn which hooks and conceptual frames actually compound.
